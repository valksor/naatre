# Code of conduct

Project participants must be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Do
not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about
others. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will
handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate
the outcome to the reporter when possible.
